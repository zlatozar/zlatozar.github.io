Warnier and Orr showed that starting from the output
hierarchy makes tricky formatting rules much easier to
organize. When a command triggers a paragraph break,
the current paragraph finishes under the old settings!
?mode justify
?paragraph 0 1
Every justified line stretches the word gaps up to the
right margin, except the last line of the paragraph.
The extra spaces go to the left gaps on one line and to
the right gaps on the next, so no river of white space
runs down one side of the text.
