## How I Think About Software

A few recurring threads run through my work and writing:

* **Output-first & data-driven design:** Whether drawing a 1970s Warnier/Orr bracket tree or defining algebraic data types, starting from what a system must produce clarifies invariants before a single loop is written.
* **Domain clarity over accidental complexity:** Modeling business concepts explicitly through Domain-Driven Design, Hexagonal Architecture, Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), and Specification-Driven Design.
* **Foundations matter:** Studying *SICP*, *PAIP*, *Thinking Mathematically*, and building compilers (such as Andrew Appel’s *Tiger* language in F#) keeps day-to-day enterprise architecture grounded in first principles.
* **Teaching & mentoring:** From supervising diploma students at TUES to leading internal Python, functional programming, and conference talks.
